## Owners

- `escort-job-records` retains actual Pidgey occupancy through completed8 before
  successful ground refresh, original posting debt, board/mail draw order and
  regular news through49.
- `escort-work` owns ordered station preparation, money/area prefix, item
  overflow, result acknowledgment and day advance. It consumes the actual guest
  objective and preserves no-turn client leave/stay dialogue.
- `escort-work-history`, `escort-station`, `escort-jobs` and `escort-ground`
  validate actual first/second claims, scene/day boundaries, source job kinds,
  source-sized batches, selected roster and guest-preserving summit state.
- `escort-work-scenes` and authored `escort-work` own inside24, distinct outside
  dispatch, original scene staging and the one real Sinister unlock receipt.
- `escort-work-prefix` carries v22/v23 debt unchanged and accepts earlier debt
  descriptors only after exact original-envelope admission by the future codec.

These are separate current-state owners. No old whole-campaign policy receives
fabricated totals, scenes, day, resources or an emptied roster/session.
`check-escort-station.mjs` parses source and checks these joins without importing
or executing any game module. Complete heritage/raw callback and live activation
remain the next dependency in the same implementation task.
