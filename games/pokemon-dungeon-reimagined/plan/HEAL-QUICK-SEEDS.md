## Heal responsibility

For a valid live current-floor recipient, `heal-quick.js` evaluates the exact
shared `hasNegativeStatus` once after impact interruption. If true, it ends
whole admitted classes in native order: Sleep, Burn, Petrified through the
accepted frozen end owner, Cringe, then recipient-only Leech and Sure Shot.
EndCringe's cache refresh precedes the later slow comparison, even when Cringe
was empty. Class ends preserve unrelated source actors and their other effects.
Focus Energy alone stays; Focus Energy with slow or a sealed move is erased
with its whole Sure Shot class. Pure positive states do not trigger the predicate.

Then, independently of that predicate, Heal calculates speed, clears all five
negative timers, recalculates and reports only an actual stage change; every
positive counter and Speed Boost counter stays. All existing learned seals are
cleared, preserving exact slots/links/SET/enabled/currentPP/power/gains/EXP.
Graph admission already joins battle slots exactly to existing learned slots.
Cache recalculation never installs a raise flag or clears attack lock; earlier
raise/deferred/pending/swap flags and the action/continuation cursor retain their
owners. Ordinary class-end, speed-recovery, seal and already-cured feedback
follows the native order and actual change.

There is no Safeguard/ability gate, cure RNG, HP/PP/stat restoration, AI reset or
inventory mutation beyond the common seed consumption. Bide/Rage/Charge,
Reflect class, Long Toss, invisibility, unrelated stages/multipliers/resources,
positive speed/growth and learned metadata remain intact. Heal never invokes
floor reset, revival or removal's `clearLeechSeedLinks` global cleanup.

Wider classes stay unadmitted and are guarded before mutation: Napping requires
HP healing and recursive cure; Nightmare/Yawning require their exact sleep ends;
Wrap/Wrapped require reciprocal release; Snatch/Decoy require global ownership;
Destiny Bond requires its user-slot end; eyesight requires leader trap/minimap
refresh. Muzzled/Perish/Exposed and remaining class end contracts also stay
separate dependencies. No empty generic end claims their closure. Later admission
must install complete source ends in sleep/burn/frozen/cringe/curse/leech/sure-shot/
eyesight/muzzled/perish/exposed order, including coexisting positive members.

## Quick responsibility

For a valid recipient, Quick draws once from `[8,10)` then adds1, producing9
or10 before any maximum-stage or full-positive-array check. It applies no
Self Curer/Natural Cure/Early Bird/Safeguard reduction or gate. The reviewed
`rules/speed.js` inserts into the first empty positive slot below stage4, retains
all negative counters and never replaces a full array. Capped/full/equal outcomes
still spend the duration draw and receive their actual unchanged feedback.

`turns/support.js:installSpeedChange` writes timers/cache, ORs a true raise into
the earlier `speedRaisedThisAction` and clears `attackLocked` only for a real
increase. It does not falsely raise an unchanged stage. Existing native eligible-
opportunity timer upkeep and leader phase refresh remain owners. The completed
Quick action yields before later refresh; consumption and duration never replay.
