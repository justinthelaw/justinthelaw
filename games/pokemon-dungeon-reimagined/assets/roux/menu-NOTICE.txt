Roux menu/title scenery, cream-and-chocolate banner and lettering are authored fan adaptations created with the offline tools/pokemon-dungeon/art/roux/menu.mjs pipeline. The dedicated title avatar is a uniformly reduced cutout of the owner-approved concept A artwork generated with image_gen from the owner's Roux reference, separate from the gameplay atlas. The upper Pokemon / Mystery Dungeon logo tiers use a documented crop of the North American English Blue Rescue Team menu export. Original game artwork: Nintendo / The Pokemon Company / Chunsoft. Source captures are credited to Mystery Dungeon Wiki; original menu-capture provenance is incomplete on the hosting wiki. Source URLs, hashes, crop and qualifications are recorded in the authoring manifest. These custom compositions do not claim original native scene or pixel parity. Public artwork and attribution do not establish a publisher reuse grant. Roux character attribution is recorded separately in NOTICE.txt.
