Blue opening original-art package

These character atlases are compact derivatives of original, source-native
character art already authored for this repository. No ROM data, Nintendo DS
sprite, commercial portrait, downloaded game art or commercial font is included.

The four cinematic PNGs are original integer-grid paintings authored in
tools/pokemon-dungeon/art/blue/intro.mjs. Original-game screenshots informed
scene layout only; their raster pixels were not copied, traced, or sampled.

Reproduce with:
  node tools/pokemon-dungeon/art/blue/export.mjs

The manifest records the exact source-page hashes, output hashes and dimensions.
Original art is not Nintendo DS visual parity. Pokemon and the depicted
characters remain the intellectual property of their respective rights holders;
original authorship of these files does not assert a rights-holder license.
